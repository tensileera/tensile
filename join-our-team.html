THE WORK

A demanding system.
Connected disciplines.

A tensile rotor is a structural and controls problem at the same time. Progress depends on engineers who can work deeply within their discipline and carefully across its interfaces.

Show us your reasoning, the constraints you worked within, and how you verified the result. A project, research contribution, fabrication record or technical portfolio can demonstrate that work.
