01

Scope and responsible organization

This Privacy Policy explains how Kunfirm Innovative Services LLC (KTX), operating the TensileEra website, handles information associated with this public site, email inquiries and expressions of interest. It does not replace a separate privacy notice or agreement for a future product or contracted service.

02

Information associated with your visit

Information you provide. If you contact us or submit an expression of interest, you may provide your name, email address, organization, message, portfolio and other material you choose to share.

Technical information. The hosting and service providers delivering the site may process information such as IP address, requested pages, browser or device information, timestamps and security logs.

Acceptance preference. The website stores the accepted Terms and Privacy Policy versions and an acceptance timestamp in your browser. The acceptance feature does not collect your name or IP address and does not transmit this browser record to a consent database.

03

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, evaluate engineering expressions of interest, discuss potential collaborations, deliver and protect the website, maintain relevant business records and meet applicable legal obligations. Please provide only information relevant to your request.

04

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be handled by service providers supporting hosting, email, security and application processing, as needed for those functions. We may disclose information where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect rights or security, or in connection with a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

This website does not contain advertising analytics, advertising pixels, embedded social feeds or data-sale features. External links do not load those services into this page.

05

Cookies and local browser storage

This implementation uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It uses a local-storage entry named tensileera.website-acceptance to remember your explicit acceptance. The entry contains policy versions and a timestamp; it is valid for up to 12 months and is removed when the site next detects expiry. A policy-version change requires a new acceptance.

You can remove the entry through your browser’s site-data controls. Clearing or blocking storage may cause the dialog to appear again. Use “Review website terms” in the footer to reopen it. Accepting website terms does not subscribe you to marketing or authorize unrelated tracking.

06

Email, applications and external services

Email links open your email application. Messages you send are processed through the email services involved and received by the team. The engineering application link opens Tally, an external service with its own privacy practices.

Review the Tally privacy notice and the privacy information of other external services before using them. Hosting-provider information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The exact providers involved may depend on the route you use.

07

Retention and security

Inquiry and application information is retained as needed for the purpose for which it was provided, relevant follow-up, legitimate recordkeeping and applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of information and the engagement; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion date for every record.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep initial inquiries non-confidential and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Contact us first if a protected exchange is required.

08

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit an inquiry, decline the Terms and leave the website, clear local site storage, or ask about information you have provided. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your information, or to obtain a copy.

Send requests to legal@tensileera.com. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant record. Applicable legal obligations and exceptions may affect a request. Where applicable, you may contact your data-protection authority.

09

Audience and international access

The website is intended for professional and business audiences and is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit children’s personal information. If you believe such information has been provided, contact us.

KTX is based in the United States. Website, email and external service providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate, subject to applicable requirements.

10

Updates and contact

We may update this policy by publishing a revised version and effective date. The website requests a fresh acknowledgment when its recorded policy version changes.

Privacy contact: legal@tensileera.com.
